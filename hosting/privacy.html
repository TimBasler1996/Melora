What Melora does

Melora lets you share the track you are currently playing on Spotify with people nearby (“going live”), see who is live around you, like their tracks, send messages and follow people. This policy explains which data that involves, why we process it, who can see it and how you stay in control.

What other users can see

  • Your first and last name, age, city, gender, what you are looking for, your photos and your follower and like counts.
  • While you are live: the track you are playing and your approximate distance from them. Live sessions stay visible as “recently live” for up to 24 hours, without a distance.
  • Never: your birthday, email, exact location, Spotify identifiers, or who you blocked or hid.

People you block cannot see you in Discover, message you or find you in search.

Services we rely on

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Storage, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging) stores and processes the data above on Google’s servers, which may be located outside your country, including in the United States. Google acts as our processor under its data processing terms.
  • Spotify provides the currently playing track and profile details when you connect your account. Spotify’s own privacy policy applies to your Spotify account.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple and push notification delivery, and MapKit for city suggestions while you type.

We do not sell your data and we do not show advertising.

How long we keep data

  • Live sessions are removed 24 hours after they end.
  • Your profile, photos, likes, messages, follows and blocks are kept until you delete your profile.
  • Reports are kept for up to 12 months after the reported profile is deleted, so we can act on repeated abuse.

Your rights and choices

  • See and change your profile in the app at any time.
  • Delete everything with Settings → Delete profile and data. This removes your profile, photos, likes, chats, follows, blocks, live session and your login within minutes.
  • Location, notifications and Spotify can each be switched off in iOS Settings or in the app; Melora keeps working without them, except that going live needs Spotify and location.
  • Depending on where you live you may also have the right to access, correct, export or object to the processing of your data, and to complain to a data protection authority. Write to [support email] and we will answer within 30 days.

Age

Melora is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly process data of anyone younger; if you believe we do, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters to you, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top shows the current version.
